# Approved page artwork

The web shell expects these exact approved page assets:

- assets/home.webp — approved Home/Landing page
- assets/fourways.webp — approved Four Ways In page
- assets/episodes.webp — approved Episodes template
- assets/swag.webp — approved Swag template
- assets/guest.webp — approved Be My Guest design
- assets/discover.webp — approved Discover design
- assets/about.webp — approved About design
- assets/contact.webp — approved Contact design

The image artwork remains the visual authority. Navigation and responsive behavior are layered around it rather than regenerating or substituting the designs.
